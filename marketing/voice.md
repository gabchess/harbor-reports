# Voice

We write like a sales manager who has built the Monday page by hand and is glad to stop.

## Rules

- Short sentences, one idea each.
- Name the person and the task. "A manager sends invites", not "invites can be sent".
- Every number says where it came from, such as "in the closed beta".
- Plain words. A report, a rep, a deal, an export.
- No exclamation marks and no hype words such as revolutionary, seamless or game changing.

## Sounds like

- "The weekly report now lists which deals slipped and the rep's reason."
- "In the closed beta, managers built the team page in 11 minutes, down from 52."

## Does not sound like

- "Supercharge your pipeline with next level insights!"
- "Harbor Reports is now blazing fast for everyone."
