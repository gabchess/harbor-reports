# Positioning

Harbor Reports turns a pipeline export into a weekly report a sales manager reads in two minutes.

## Who it is for

Sales managers of small teams, from 3 to 15 reps, who build a Monday pipeline page for their director. Founders who run a small sales team themselves.

## The problem we name

The weekly page is built by hand. A manager collects exports, pastes them into a sheet and works out which deals moved. The sheet holds totals, so nobody can say which deal slipped or why.

## What we claim

- One weekly page from the export a team already has.
- Deals that moved and deals that slipped, each with its rep.
- No admin setup and no sheet.

## What we never claim

- A speed or a time saving without a measurement behind it.
- Results from a beta as results for all customers.
- Anything about price or plans before product confirms it.
